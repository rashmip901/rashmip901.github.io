Your data, explained

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Perfect 100 is an Android skill-challenge game developed and operated by Rashmi Pandey. This policy explains how the app handles information, including local game records, Google advertising services and information you choose to share or send to support.

For privacy questions, contact rashmip901@gmail.com.

At a glance

Your challenge history and settings stay on your device. There is no Perfect 100 account or gameplay-data backend. The app includes Google advertising and consent services that may process information off-device. Result sharing is your choice.

1. Game history and local settings

Perfect 100 stores challenge results, raw performance measurements, scores, protocol versions, trial configurations, seeds, personal bests and Daily Challenge records in app-private storage on your device. These records support results, Details, history and personal progress. App code does not upload these records to a Perfect 100 server or include them in advertising requests.

The app also stores preferences such as haptics, sound, reduced decorative motion, selected mode, onboarding and tutorial progress. Local advertising counters help limit ad frequency. A small, bounded local record of interface events supports app diagnostics; there is no developer-operated remote analytics exporter.

History summaries remain until you delete them. Storage for detailed raw sample archives is bounded, so older detailed samples may be removed while their result summaries remain. Performance history and player preferences are excluded from the app's configured Android cloud-backup and device-transfer rules. Perfect 100 does not provide cloud recovery or synchronization. Google SDK-managed data may have separate handling.

2. Google advertising and consent services

The app includes Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). These services can connect to Google even though core gameplay and local history work offline. Ad availability is not required to play the core challenges. There are no in-app purchases offered in this release.

Google describes its Mobile Ads SDK as processing IP addresses, app interactions, diagnostic information and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. An IP address can be used to estimate general location. Available identifiers and processing depend on Android, SDK configuration and applicable privacy choices. The absence of an advertising-ID permission does not mean that all SDK identifiers or network data are disabled.

UMP processes consent-related requests and manages applicable privacy choices. Consent information may be refreshed before an ad request. Where required, the app presents Google's consent interface and a Privacy choices control so you can review your choices. Refusing personalized advertising does not necessarily eliminate every form of SDK data processing.

Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure describes its data categories and encrypted transport. See also Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information for advertising. Google controls its own service retention and applicable data-rights processes; deleting local game history does not delete information already processed by Google.

3. Sharing results

If you choose Share, the app creates a result image in private temporary cache and opens Android's sharing interface. The image can contain the challenge name, useful result, score, tier, Daily date and branding. It does not contain internal trial arrays, protocol JSON, device identifiers or database IDs.

The app grants the selected receiving app temporary access to the image. That app handles any further storage or transmission under its own policies. Perfect 100 cannot remove copies already sent to another person or service. Stale share images are eligible for cleanup after 24 hours; Android may clear cache earlier.

4. Support messages and diagnostics

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments are received through the developer's Gmail account and used to respond to your request and investigate reported problems. Only include information you want to send. You may contact the same address about deletion of your correspondence; handling is subject to applicable legal requirements and the email provider's retention processes.

The app does not include a separate developer-operated remote crash-reporting SDK. Google Play may provide platform crash and performance reports, and Google's advertising SDK can process its own diagnostics. These are distinct from your on-device challenge history.

5. Permissions and security

The app uses network access for Google advertising and consent services. Some included libraries declare background-service, wake-lock and dormant billing permissions; no purchase flow is enabled. Perfect 100 does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, notifications or shared photo/media library.

Local records are protected by Android's app storage controls. Google's Mobile Ads documentation describes encryption in transit for its SDK data. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure, and a recipient app's sharing behavior is outside Perfect 100's control.

6. Your choices and deletion

  • Clear history: use the confirmed history-deletion control in Profile to remove local attempts and Daily history.
  • Reset profile: removes local history and resets player preferences and the local interface-event record. Separately stored tutorial and advertising/consent state may remain.
  • Clear app storage or uninstall: removes app-private local data. This does not delete shared copies, support email or data already processed by third-party services.
  • Feedback preferences: change haptics, sound and decorative-motion preferences in the app.
  • Advertising choices: use the in-app Privacy choices entry when provided and relevant Android or Google privacy controls.

No Perfect 100 account is required, so there is no app account to delete. For questions or applicable privacy-rights requests, email rashmip901@gmail.com. The developer cannot retrieve on-device history from a remote server because no such history service exists.

7. Children

Perfect 100 is not designed specifically for children. It does not ask players to create an account or provide their date of birth. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about information sent to the developer, contact the email address below. Google advertising and consent services also have their own policies and applicable age-related rules.

8. This website and external services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no developer-added analytics, tracking scripts or advertising. GitHub may process technical information when serving the pages under its Privacy Statement. Following external links or sending an email involves the selected service's own information handling.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its data handling changes. The latest version will appear on this page with an updated date.
